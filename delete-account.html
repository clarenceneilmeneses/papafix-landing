Account & data

Delete your PapaFix account

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your PapaFix account and the personal data attached to it. PapaFix is the app and developer name shown on our Google Play store listing. The request is free, and you do not need to be signed in to make it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at clarenceneilpamplona@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my PapaFix account.
  2. In the message, include the mobile number and email address on the account, and tell us whether it is a customer or technician account. We need these to find the right account and to confirm the request is really coming from you.
  3. Reply to our confirmation email. We will write back within 3 working days to verify it is you. Deletion only goes ahead once you confirm — this protects you from someone else deleting your account.
  4. We delete the account and email you when it is done, within 30 days of your confirmation.

Deleting your account is permanent. You lose your booking history, saved addresses, and — for technicians — your rating and job record. You can sign up again later, but nothing from the old account can be restored.

Finish or settle open jobs first. If you have a booking in progress, an unpaid balance, or an open dispute, we will tell you and hold the deletion until that job is closed. Everything else in your request still goes ahead as described below.

What gets deleted

Once your request is processed, we permanently delete:

  • Your account details — name, mobile number, email address, and login credentials.
  • Your profile photo.
  • Your saved service addresses.
  • Your location data, including any stored trip or arrival-tracking history.
  • Job notes and photos you attached to bookings.
  • Your support conversations with our team.
  • Your push-notification and device identifiers, so the app can no longer reach your phone.
  • For technician accounts, your verification records — government ID and proof of trade.
  • Your email address on the launch waitlist, if it is still there.

Reviews and ratings you wrote are stripped of your name and any detail that identifies you, so the technician's overall score stays accurate without pointing back to you.

What we keep, and for how long

Philippine law requires us to hold on to a small amount of information even after your account is gone. None of it is used to market to you or to rebuild your profile:

  • Transaction records — the date, service, amount, and receipt number of jobs you paid for. Philippine tax and accounting rules require businesses to preserve books of accounts and supporting records for up to 10 years. These records are kept without your name or contact details, tied only to an internal reference.
  • Records tied to an open matter — an unresolved dispute, unpaid balance, insurance claim, or legal request. Kept until the matter is closed, then deleted.
  • Safety and fraud records — if an account was suspended for fraud, or for conduct that put a customer or technician at risk, we keep a minimal record so the same person cannot simply re-register. Kept for 5 years.
  • Aggregate statistics — counts such as total jobs completed in a city. These are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you, so they are kept indefinitely.

Deleted data also lingers in our encrypted backups for a short window while old backups age out. Backups are overwritten on a rolling 90-day cycle, after which no copy remains.

Delete some data but keep your account

You do not have to delete everything. Email the same address and tell us what you want removed — a saved address, your profile photo, your location history — and we will do that without closing the account.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to access, correct, object to the processing of, and request the erasure or blocking of your personal information, and to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. We respond to all requests within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture of what we collect and why.
